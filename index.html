Featured favourites

Most popular air-fryer dishes

Classic

Crispy Chicken Nuggets

  • Chicken breast or nuggets
  • Breadcrumbs or panko
  • Salt, pepper, paprika
Method

Coat the nuggets lightly, spray with oil, and air fry at 200°C for 10–12 minutes, turning once.

Crowd pleaser

Golden Chips

  • Potatoes cut into chips
  • Olive oil or spray oil
  • Sea salt and black pepper
Method

Soak the chips briefly, dry well, toss in oil, and cook at 200°C for 15–20 minutes, shaking halfway.

Quick bite

Air-Fried Chicken Wings

  • Chicken wings
  • Garlic powder
  • Hot sauce or BBQ glaze
Method

Season well, air fry at 190°C for 18–22 minutes, then toss in sauce for extra flavour.

Healthy option

Air-Fried Salmon Fillets

  • Salmon fillets
  • Lemon juice
  • Herbs and garlic
Method

Brush with oil and seasoning, cook at 180°C for 10–12 minutes until flaky and golden.

Cheesy

Air-Fried Cheese Toasties

  • Bread
  • Cheddar or mozzarella
  • Butter or garlic spread
Method

Build the sandwich, air fry at 180°C for 6–8 minutes until crisp and melty.

Special

Air-Fried Trangoli Pasta

  • Cooked pasta shapes
  • Tomato sauce or pesto
  • Cheese and herbs
Method

Mix pasta with sauce, place in a shallow dish, top with cheese, and air fry at 180°C until crisp at the edges and piping hot.

Air-fryer basics

Little tricks for perfect results

1. Don’t overcrowd

Leave space around each piece so the hot air can circulate and crisp everything evenly.

2. Spray lightly

A quick spray of oil helps with colour and crunch without making food greasy.

3. Shake or turn

Flip chips, nuggets, or wings halfway through for that perfect golden finish.

4. Preheat when needed

For extra crispness, preheat the air fryer for a few minutes before cooking.
